# Data Notice

This is primarily a mathematical foundations paper. Its main text now carries
a compact public-data verdict; the complete audit is preserved in the
separately compiled technical supplement under
`paperVI_submission_source/public_data_audit_supplement.tex`. The audit uses
compact frozen QGT figure extractions and the six Figure 2 tables from
Ness et al. (2021) needed to reproduce a standard quantum-speed-limit control.
Source identifiers, roles and claim boundaries are recorded under
`data/public_qgt/`.

The files under `data/derived/` are reproducibility artifacts generated by the
included audits. The public controls support standard quantum geometry and
standard speed-limit consistency. They are not empirical evidence for Tau Core
or for a deviation from quantum mechanics, because no dataset contains an
independently reconstructed morphological action ratio `x_A`.

The package also includes the CC0 Dryad tables of Wen et al. (2026), DOI
`10.5061/dryad.x0k6djj14`, under `data/public_optical_path_integral/`. The
committed CSV copies reproduce the action--phase and equal-amplitude controls.
They are aggregate figure tables and do not provide joint path-level
morphology, action and amplitude records; they therefore do not support a
conditional Tau morphology score.

The archived `FigS2B.xlsx` table is retained as an independent aggregate
radial-phase control. It contains 17 experimental phase points and a 200-point
theory curve, but no same-index complex amplitude, recovery record or
independent Tau body-action axis.

The companion `FigS2C.xlsx` and `FigS2D.xlsx` files provide the authors'
published normalized-amplitude histograms, means and dispersions for the same
17 radial propagator classes. They replace the failed image-level amplitude
reduction for the bounded morphology diagnostic. Their result remains a
standard optical-kernel statistic, not an independently measured Tau action.
