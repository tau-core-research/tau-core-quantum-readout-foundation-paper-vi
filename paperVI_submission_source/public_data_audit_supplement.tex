\begin{abstract}
This supplement preserves the full candidate census, negative controls,
source-port audits, finite countermodels and readiness scoring underlying the
compact public-data verdict in Paper VI.  It does not add a new Tau detection.
Its central result is an identifiability no-go: no audited public carrier
simultaneously supplies an independent once-counted action-side observable,
an Uhlmann-capable endpoint record and the required recovery and holonomy
ledger.  All scores are capability or eligibility scores, not signal
significances.
\end{abstract}

\paragraph{Dependency.}
Equation and theorem references prefixed by the main-paper numbering refer to
Paper VI.  This supplement owns only the detailed audit ledger and its finite
controls; it does not strengthen the main paper's conditional claims.
