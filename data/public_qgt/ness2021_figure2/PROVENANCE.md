# Ness et al. 2021 Figure 2 data

Source article: D. Ness et al., *Observing crossover between quantum speed
limits*, Science Advances 7, eabj9119 (2021), DOI:
`10.1126/sciadv.abj9119`.

The six tab-separated files in this directory are copied without numerical
modification from `sciadv.abj9119_data_s1.zip`, distributed as an associated
supplementary file by Europe PMC for `PMC8694601`:

`https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC8694601/supplementaryFiles`

Only the three Figure 2 measured tables and their corresponding fitted/theory
tables are retained. They contain time, measured overlap and asymmetric
intervals, plus the fitted overlap and the Mandelstam--Tamm and
Margolus--Levitin lower-limit curves with intervals.

This package is used only as a public standard-quantum-speed-limit control. It
does not provide an independently reconstructed Tau morphological action
separation and therefore cannot test `x_A = x_Q`.
